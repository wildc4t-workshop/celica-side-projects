**Important:** the project continued after the final published post. The later undocumented Wilwood Superlite + Corvette rotor front architecture and LS430 + Nissan rotor rear work in [`PROJECT.md`](PROJECT.md) supersede conflicting published conclusions.

## Historical design progression captured in the blog

The published work explored multiple caliper/rotor combinations including:

- TRD/tC caliper concepts;
- Cadillac ATS/CTS-V/XTS Brembo concepts;
- Durango/Cherokee and Evo X rotor options;
- Wilwood Superlite investigation;
- multiple 3D-printed/calculated adapter iterations.

The blog should be treated as **design-history evidence**, not as the current released architecture.

## Current authoritative checkpoint

Use [`PROJECT.md`](PROJECT.md) for the later undocumented state:

- Wilwood Superlite + redrilled Corvette rotor selected for the front.
- Printed front adapter physically fit-checked.
- Metal adapter awaiting structural verification.
- LS430 + redrilled Nissan rear architecture technically viable but not accepted because of undesirable custom hub/adapter work.
- Brake balance and master-cylinder sizing still require recovery/verification.
